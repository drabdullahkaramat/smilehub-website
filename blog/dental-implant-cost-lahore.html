Dental Implant Cost in Lahore 2026 — Complete Price Guide

Dr. Abdullah Karamat
BDS, FCPS · Certified Implantologist

The cost of dental implants in Lahore is one of the most searched questions among patients who are considering replacing a missing tooth. The honest answer is: it depends — on the surgeon's qualification, the implant brand, whether bone grafting is needed, and the type of crown placed on top.

This guide breaks down everything you need to know about implant pricing in Lahore, what drives the cost, and how to evaluate whether you are getting good value — written by a Certified Implantologist who performs these procedures regularly.

What Does the Cost Actually Include?

A dental implant is not a single item — it is a multi-component restoration involving several appointments. Understanding what you are paying for helps you compare quotes fairly.

The Implant Fixture

The titanium screw that is placed surgically into the jawbone. Premium implant brands (Nobel Biocare, Straumann, Osstem) are internationally tested with decades of clinical data. Budget implants from unknown manufacturers carry significantly higher failure risk — the difference in cost is real, but so is the difference in quality.

The Abutment

The connector piece that links the implant fixture to the crown. Should match the implant system — using mismatched components is a common cost-cutting shortcut that compromises the long-term result.

The Crown

The visible tooth restoration placed on top of the implant. Zirconia crowns are the gold standard — strong, aesthetic, and biocompatible. PFM (metal-ceramic) crowns are less expensive but inferior for anterior (front) teeth.

Surgical Fees

The professional fee for implant placement surgery, including local anaesthesia, the surgical appointment, and follow-up visits. This component reflects the surgeon's qualification and experience — the most important variable in implant success.

What Factors Affect the Total Cost?

1. The Surgeon's Qualification

This is the most important factor — and the one most patients overlook when comparing prices. Implant placement is a surgical procedure. A Certified Implantologist has completed dedicated surgical training in implant protocols beyond general dental qualification. An FCPS prosthodontist who also holds an implant certification combines surgical skill with the specialist knowledge required to design the final restoration correctly.

At Smile Hub Clinics, all implants are placed by Dr. Abdullah Karamat — BDS, FCPS, Certified Implantologist. He both places the implant and designs the final restoration — ensuring seamless coordination between surgery and the aesthetic outcome.

2. Bone Volume

Sufficient jawbone is required to support an implant. If bone has been lost — due to long-term tooth loss, gum disease, or infection — a bone graft may be needed before or at the time of implant placement. Bone grafting adds PKR 30,000–80,000 to the cost depending on the extent required.

3. Implant Brand

Premium implant systems cost more than budget alternatives — but the difference in osseointegration rates, long-term stability, and component availability is clinically significant. A failed implant that needs to be removed and replaced costs far more than the premium brand would have.

4. Position in the Mouth

Front teeth implants require more aesthetic precision and typically cost slightly more than posterior (back tooth) implants. Upper back teeth near the sinus may require a sinus lift procedure if bone height is insufficient.

5. Number of Implants

Multiple implants are sometimes discounted compared to individual pricing. Full arch rehabilitation with implants is priced very differently from single tooth replacement — see our Full Mouth Rehabilitation page for details.

⚠️ A Note on Very Low-Cost Implants

Implants advertised at PKR 30,000–40,000 complete often involve unbranded fixtures with unknown surface treatment, performed by operators without dedicated implant training. Implant failure is significantly higher in these cases. When an implant fails, the cost of removal, bone grafting, healing time, and replacement far exceeds the original saving. For a procedure intended to last a lifetime, quality and qualification are the most important variables.

How much does a dental implant cost in Lahore?
A single dental implant in Lahore typically costs between PKR 80,000 and PKR 180,000 at a specialist clinic with a Certified Implantologist — including the titanium fixture, abutment, and zirconia crown. Budget clinics may quote lower but often use unbranded components with higher failure rates.

How long does the full implant process take?
From first consultation to final crown, a single implant typically takes 3–6 months — most of which is the osseointegration healing period (2–4 months) rather than active treatment. The surgical appointment itself is 45–60 minutes.
Does insurance cover dental implants in Pakistan?
Most dental insurance plans in Pakistan do not cover implants, as they are classified as elective restorative treatment. Some corporate health schemes include partial dental coverage — check your specific policy. We provide detailed cost breakdowns to support any insurance claims.
