About

Background & Experience

Dr. Abdullah Karamat is an FCPS-qualified Prosthodontist, Certified Implantologist, and Certified Aligners Provider based in Lahore, Pakistan. He is the co-founder of Smile Hub Clinics in Johar Town, Lahore, where he leads implant dentistry, full mouth rehabilitation, and cosmetic smile design.

Alongside his clinical practice, Dr. Abdullah holds a position as Assistant Professor of Dentistry, where he teaches and mentors the next generation of dental graduates in prosthodontic principles and restorative techniques.

His clinical focus centres on combining surgical precision with prosthetic design — placing dental implants and designing the final restoration as part of one coordinated treatment plan, rather than separating surgery and aesthetics into disconnected stages. This approach is reflected throughout the implant, veneer, and smile makeover cases treated at Smile Hub Clinics.

Dr. Abdullah writes regularly on dental health topics for patients in Lahore — covering everything from the realities of root canal treatment to the true cost considerations behind dental implants — with the goal of helping patients make informed decisions before they ever sit in the dental chair.

Dr. Karamat's qualifications, teaching, and clinical focus are set out in more detail at drabdullahkaramat.com.

The Patients I Enjoy Treating Most

The patients I enjoy treating most are the curious ones — the ones who ask "but why?" and lean in when I pull up the X-ray to show them. And the nervous ones, who become curious once they realise nobody here is going to push them into anything.

Many come in guarded, because somewhere along the way a dentist sold them something they didn't need — and I watch them relax the first time I say "honestly, you don't need this." That's usually the moment we become a team.

They think long-term, like I do: they'd rather hear the truth about how long veneers really last than a comfortable exaggeration, and they'd rather fix the real problem than keep patching around it. They sleep easier knowing there's a plan B — a warranty behind the work, a record of exactly what's in their mouth, someone to call even after they've flown home. And they do their part, because they understand we're building something together — their health, my work, one trust at a time.

Many of them have been with me for years now. Their kids too. That's the practice I'm building — one patient like this at a time.
