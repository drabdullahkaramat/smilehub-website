Lead Specialist & Co-Founder

Dr. Abdullah Karamat

Prosthodontist & Implantologist
BDS FCPS Certified Implantologist Certified Aligners Provider Assistant Professor

Dr. Abdullah Karamat is a prosthodontist and implantologist with postgraduate training from one of Pakistan's leading dental institutions. His Fellowship of the College of Physicians and Surgeons (FCPS) is in Prosthodontics — the specialty dedicated to dental aesthetics, implants, and full-mouth reconstruction.

Beyond clinical practice, Dr. Abdullah serves as Assistant Professor of Dentistry, actively contributing to the training of the next generation of dental professionals. He co-founded Smile Hub Clinics in 2021 with a single mission: to bring specialist-level dental care to Johar Town.
