What Are Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners are a series of custom-fabricated, removable plastic trays that gradually move your teeth into their correct positions. Each set of aligners is worn for approximately one to two weeks before progressing to the next, with each tray applying precise, controlled pressure to specific teeth.

Unlike traditional braces, aligners are nearly invisible, can be removed for eating and cleaning, and cause significantly less discomfort. For patients who want straighter teeth without the social and practical inconvenience of metal braces, clear aligners are often the ideal solution — provided they are prescribed and monitored by a certified provider.

What Can Aligners Treat?

Clear aligners are highly effective for a wide range of orthodontic cases. At your consultation, we will assess your specific situation and advise whether aligners are the right solution:

🦷

Crowding

Teeth that overlap or are too close together due to insufficient space in the jaw.

↔️

Spacing

Gaps between teeth caused by missing teeth, abnormal jaw growth, or small teeth.

⬆️

Overbite

Upper front teeth extend too far over the lower front teeth vertically.

⬇️

Underbite

Lower teeth protrude in front of the upper teeth when biting. Mild to moderate cases.

↕️

Crossbite

Some upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth instead of outside, causing uneven wear.

🔄

Open Bite

Upper and lower teeth don't meet when the mouth is closed — common in thumb-suckers.

Your Aligner Journey at Smile Hub Clinics

1

Free Consultation & Assessment

A clinical examination of your teeth, bite, and jaw. We take photographs, X-rays, and impressions or digital scans to assess your case. You'll receive an honest recommendation on whether aligners are suitable for your specific situation.

2

Treatment Planning

Using your scans and impressions, a detailed treatment plan is created showing the projected movement of your teeth from start to finish. You'll be able to see a preview of your expected final result before committing to treatment.

3

Custom Aligner Fabrication

Your aligners are precision-manufactured based on your unique dental anatomy. Each tray in the series is slightly different, applying controlled force to move specific teeth at each stage of treatment.

4

Fitting & Instructions

Your first set of aligners is fitted and you receive full instructions — how to insert and remove them, cleaning protocol, wear schedule, and what to expect in the first few days. Small tooth-coloured attachments may be bonded to certain teeth to help the aligners grip.

5

Progress Monitoring

Check-up appointments every 6–8 weeks allow your provider to monitor tooth movement, address any issues, and hand over the next sets of aligners. Compliance is the most important factor — aligners must be worn 20–22 hours per day for treatment to stay on track.

6

Retainers & Completion

Once treatment is complete, retainers are provided to maintain your new smile. Teeth naturally want to shift back — retainers prevent this. They are typically worn nightly long-term to preserve your result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do clear aligners really work?
Yes. For appropriate cases — crowding, spacing, and many bite issues — clear aligners are a well-established, evidence-based way to move teeth predictably. The two biggest factors in success are correct case selection by a certified provider and your compliance: aligners only work when worn 20–22 hours a day. Poor case selection or inconsistent wear are the main reasons treatment falls short.
What is the cost of clear aligners in Lahore, Pakistan?
At Smile Hub Clinics, clear aligners cost between PKR 150,000 and 180,000 as of 2026, depending on case complexity. The price is all-inclusive — scans, aligners, refinements, retainers, and review visits — with no hidden charges. Payment is split: half upfront and half spread over the following year. The initial consultation, including a 3D simulation of your result, is free.
Am I too old for clear aligners?
No — there is no upper age limit. Teeth can be moved safely at any age as long as your gums and supporting bone are healthy. Many of our aligner patients are working professionals in their 30s, 40s, and beyond. Gum health, not age, determines suitability.
How many hours a day do I need to wear them?
20–22 hours a day — essentially all the time except when eating, drinking anything but water, and brushing. A good rule is to keep them out for no longer than about 30 minutes at a stretch. Consistent wear is the single biggest factor in finishing on schedule; frequent removal stalls tooth movement.
Can I eat, drink, and kiss with aligners in?
Remove them to eat and to drink anything other than plain water — food and coloured drinks stain or warp the trays, and chewing can crack them. Always brush before reinserting. Kissing with aligners in is completely fine; they're thin, clear, and barely noticeable.
Do aligners hurt?
You may feel mild pressure or tenderness for a day or two each time you move to a new set — this is normal and means the teeth are moving. It's generally far more comfortable than the tightening of metal braces, and pain relief is rarely needed.
Are clear aligners as effective as braces?
For mild to moderate crowding, spacing, and many bite issues, aligners produce results comparable to braces while being far more discreet and comfortable. For severe crowding, significant skeletal bite problems, or complex rotations, fixed braces or a combined approach may give a better result — and we'll tell you honestly if that's the case for you.
Are clear aligners permissible in Islam?
Clear aligners are removable and non-permanent, and they correct teeth into a healthier, more functional position rather than permanently altering natural creation for vanity alone — which is why many scholars regard corrective orthodontic treatment as permissible. This is general information rather than a religious ruling; for a personal ruling we'd encourage you to consult a scholar you trust.
Will I need retainers, and will my teeth move again years later?
Yes — retainers are essential. Teeth naturally drift back toward their old positions over the years (relapse), which is the most common reason people lose their results long after treatment. Custom retainers worn nightly, long-term, keep your smile stable. At Smile Hub, retainers are included in your cost.
How long does treatment take?
Most cases take 6 to 18 months depending on complexity — simple cases can finish in around 6 months, moderate cases typically 12–18. Consistent 20–22 hour daily wear is what keeps treatment on schedule.
Can I have aligner treatment if I live abroad?
Yes. We scan, plan, and start treatment during your visit to Lahore, then monitor remotely with photo check-ins and ship new or corrective aligners to you anywhere in the world. Many overseas patients complete most of their treatment from home, returning only if a stage requires it.
