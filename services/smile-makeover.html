What Is a Smile Makeover?

A smile makeover is a comprehensive, planned approach to improving the appearance of your smile using one or more cosmetic and restorative dental procedures. Unlike a single treatment, it begins with understanding what you dislike about your current smile, what your aesthetic goals are, and what your dental health allows — and then building a precise treatment plan to get you there.

The key distinction of a true smile makeover is that it is sequenced correctly. Whitening before veneers. Alignment before bonding. Health before aesthetics. The order of treatment matters enormously — and getting it wrong wastes both money and irreplaceable tooth structure. This is where a prosthodontist's training is invaluable.

What Concerns Can a Smile Makeover Address?

The Smile Makeover Journey

1

Smile Design Consultation

A thorough conversation about your concerns, goals, and lifestyle. Clinical photographs are taken and your teeth, bite, and gums are examined. We discuss what's possible, what's realistic, and the most conservative route to your goal.

2

Treatment Planning

A written treatment plan is prepared outlining every procedure, the sequence, approximate timeline, and complete cost. Nothing proceeds without your understanding and approval. There are no hidden steps and no surprises mid-treatment.

3

Foundation First — Health Before Aesthetics

Any active decay, gum disease, or infection is treated before cosmetic work begins. Building aesthetics on an unhealthy foundation is one of the most common causes of cosmetic treatment failure. We never skip this step.

4

Alignment & Whitening (if needed)

Where aligners are part of the plan, they are completed before any bonded restorations. Whitening is done before veneers or crowns so that the shade of restorations can be matched to the final whitened colour. This sequencing is critical.

5

Restorative & Cosmetic Procedures

Veneers, crowns, implants, bonding, and other restorations are completed in the planned sequence. For veneers, the temporary stage provides a preview and the opportunity to refine before permanent bonding.

6

Finalisation & Long-Term Care

A comprehensive review ensures everything looks, feels, and functions as planned. Maintenance instructions, retainers if needed, and a long-term care plan are provided. Your result is designed to last — with appropriate care.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a smile makeover take?
It depends entirely on what your plan involves. A simple makeover — whitening plus bonding — may be complete in 2–3 appointments over a few weeks. A comprehensive plan involving aligners, implants, and veneers may take 12–18 months. At your consultation, Dr. Abdullah will give you a realistic timeline based on your specific case.
How much does a smile makeover cost in Lahore?
Cost varies significantly depending on which treatments are involved, how many teeth are being addressed, and the materials used. A full written breakdown is provided at your consultation before you commit to anything. We offer phased treatment plans for patients who prefer to spread the investment over time.
Will a smile makeover look natural?
When designed by a prosthodontist, yes — absolutely. Natural-looking results require understanding dental aesthetics beyond just "white teeth": the correct incisal edge position, tooth proportions relative to facial width, gum architecture, and how the teeth interact with the lips when speaking and smiling. Generic or template-driven approaches produce generic results. Personalised design produces natural ones.
Can a smile makeover fix previous bad dental work?
Yes — this is one of the most common reasons patients come to us for a smile makeover. Veneers that look artificial, crowns that don't match, or a mismatched smile after piecemeal treatment elsewhere can all be redesigned and corrected. The first consultation will assess what exists and what's involved in achieving the result you want.
Do I need to do everything at once?
Not necessarily. Many patients begin with the highest-priority concerns and complete the remaining treatments over subsequent months. We plan the full picture upfront so that each phase is done in the correct sequence — even if the timeline is extended. What matters is that treatments are sequenced properly, not that they're all rushed into one period.
Is a smile makeover painful?
Individual procedures within a makeover plan each carry their own comfort profile — implant placement, for example, is done under local anaesthesia, while whitening typically causes no discomfort at all. Your comfort at every stage is managed. Patients who have completed full makeovers at Smile Hub consistently describe the process as far more comfortable than they anticipated.
