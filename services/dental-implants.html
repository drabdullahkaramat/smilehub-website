What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium screw that is surgically placed into the jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root. Once the implant integrates with the bone — a process called osseointegration — a custom-made crown is attached on top, giving you a tooth that looks, feels, and functions exactly like a natural one.

Unlike dentures or bridges, implants don't rely on adjacent teeth for support. They stand independently, preserving your jawbone and maintaining your facial structure over time. This is what makes implants the gold standard in tooth replacement — not just cosmetically, but biologically.

Am I a Good Candidate?

Most adults with one or more missing teeth are suitable candidates for dental implants. A thorough assessment at Smile Hub Clinics will confirm your suitability. Here's a general guide:

✓ Good Candidates

  • Adults with one or more missing teeth
  • Sufficient jawbone density
  • Healthy gums (no active gum disease)
  • Non-smokers or those willing to quit
  • Good general health
  • Commitment to oral hygiene

! Requires Assessment

  • Heavy smokers
  • Uncontrolled diabetes
  • Low bone density (may need grafting)
  • Patients on blood thinners
  • Severe bruxism (teeth grinding)
  • Growing teenagers (under 18)

Even if you fall into the "requires assessment" category, it doesn't necessarily mean implants aren't possible. Bone grafting and other preparatory procedures can often make implant placement viable.

The Implant Process at Smile Hub Clinics

We follow a meticulous, staged approach to ensure the best possible outcome for every patient:

1

Free Consultation & Assessment

A thorough clinical examination including X-rays or CBCT scan to assess bone volume, gum health, and overall suitability. You'll receive a complete treatment plan with timeline and costs before any procedure begins.

2

Preparatory Treatment (if needed)

Any existing infections, gum disease, or bone deficiencies are addressed before implant placement. This ensures the implant has the healthiest possible environment to integrate into.

3

Implant Placement

The titanium fixture is placed into the jawbone under local anaesthesia. The procedure is typically completed in 45–60 minutes and is far more comfortable than most patients expect. A temporary restoration may be placed the same day.

4

Osseointegration (Healing Phase)

Over 3–6 months, the titanium implant fuses with your jawbone — a process called osseointegration. This is what gives implants their extraordinary stability and longevity. You'll have check-ups during this period.

5

Crown Placement

Once integration is confirmed, an abutment is placed and a custom-fabricated crown — matched precisely to the shape and shade of your natural teeth — is permanently fixed on top. The result is indistinguishable from a natural tooth.

6

Aftercare & Long-Term Maintenance

Implants require the same care as natural teeth — brushing, flossing, and regular dental check-ups. With proper maintenance, a well-placed implant can last a lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do dental implants cost in Lahore?
A single dental implant with abutment starts from PKR 80,000 with our economical Izen system and up to PKR 130,000 with the premium BioHorizon system. The crown on top is additional — PKR 20,000 for a PFM crown or PKR 35,000 for a zirconia crown per unit (implant crowns cost a little more than crowns on natural teeth). Any bone graft (around PKR 35,000 per gram of bone used) or membrane (from PKR 30,000) is only charged if your case needs it, so you pay for exactly what you require. You receive a full itemised breakdown at your free consultation, with no hidden costs.
Are cheap dental implants safe?
Be cautious of unusually cheap implants. When the price looks too good, ask how the dentist is cutting the cost — it is almost always at the patient's expense, through a low-grade implant, a rushed protocol, or skipped diagnostics. Our prices are deliberately reasonable without compromising the implant, the planning, or the specialist placing it.
Who places and restores the implant, and why does it matter?
Dr. Abdullah Karamat, a prosthodontist, both places and restores your implant in-house. This matters because a good implant is prosthetically driven — positioned for the final tooth, not just the available bone. When one specialist plans the crown and places the implant, it fits and functions better. Many clinics place the implant then send you elsewhere for the crown; here it is one specialist and one plan.
What implant systems do you use?
We place Izen as a reliable economical option and BioHorizon as our premium option — both established implant systems. Honestly, the system matters less than who places it: success depends far more on accurate planning and surgical skill than on the brand. We help you choose the option that suits your case and budget.
Are dental implants painful?
Placement is done under local anaesthesia, so you feel pressure, not pain. Wherever possible we use flapless, guided surgery, which is gentler and heals faster. Most patients are surprised how comfortable it is. Afterwards, any discomfort is usually mild and settles with simple pain relief over 2–3 days.
What if I am anxious about the surgery?
We don't offer IV sedation, but for nervous patients we can recommend pre-operative oral sedation on a case-by-case basis, and we follow an anxiety-reduction protocol throughout the appointment. Flapless, guided surgery also keeps the procedure calmer and quicker. Tell us beforehand and we will plan around your comfort.
How long does the whole implant process take?
From placement to the final crown, most cases take 3–6 months, allowing the implant to fuse with the bone. The surgery itself takes under an hour. In selected cases we offer immediate loading — a temporary tooth on the same day — which we assess at your consultation.
Can I still get implants if I have bone loss?
Often, yes. Where bone is insufficient, a graft — with a membrane if needed — can rebuild the site before or during placement. We use CBCT 3D scanning to measure your bone precisely and plan safely, and you only pay for the graft material your case needs. This makes implants possible for many patients who were told they couldn't have them.
How long do dental implants last?
A well-placed, well-maintained implant can last 20 years or more, often a lifetime. Published studies show success rates of roughly 95–98% over ten years in healthy patients. The titanium implant itself rarely fails; the crown on top may need replacing after 10–15 years with normal wear.
What could make an implant fail, and what happens if it does?
The main risk factors are smoking and uncontrolled diabetes, which slow healing and lower success — when you follow aftercare, failure is uncommon. And if an implant fails to integrate with no patient neglect, we redo it free of cost, because we stand behind our work.
Do I have to pay for everything upfront?
No. Payment is staged — the implant cost is due at placement, and the crown or prosthesis cost when it is fitted, often months later. For genuine cases this can be relaxed further. We will set out the full plan and timing at your free consultation.
